# Code of Conduct

This project welcomes suggestions, questions and strong opinions, but they need to be useful to the work. The aim is to improve shared resources for responsible AI use in biomedical research, not to win arguments.

## Expected behaviour

- Be constructive: explain what should change and why.
- Be specific: point to the resource, claim, example or wording you are commenting on.
- Be evidence-aware: distinguish evidence, experience, opinion and uncertainty.
- Be respectful: challenge ideas without attacking people.
- Be transparent: declare relevant conflicts, institutional constraints or limits to your expertise when they matter.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Personal attacks, harassment or discriminatory comments.
- Dismissive feedback without explanation.
- Sharing confidential, sensitive or identifiable data in issues, examples or discussions.
- Presenting speculation as established evidence.
- Repeatedly derailing discussion after a maintainer has asked to refocus.

## Sensitive examples

Do not include patient-identifiable data, unpublished confidential material, private institutional information or restricted research data in GitHub issues. If a real example involves sensitive information, describe the decision problem in general terms.

## Maintainer response

Maintainers may edit, close or remove contributions that do not follow these expectations. When possible, the preferred response is to ask for clarification or revision before rejecting a contribution.
